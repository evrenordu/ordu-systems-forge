# Evren Ordu: The System Builder

Create a premium, cinematic, high-impact personal brand website for Evren Ordu, intended for evrenordu.com. The site must feel like a blend of executive leadership, AI transformation, entrepreneurship, engineering discipline, and visual storytelling. Use a sophisticated dark navy / black / steel palette with restrained electric-blue accents, subtle motion, strong typography, and lots of contrast. Avoid generic corporate stock-site aesthetics.

Build a responsive one-page site with sections and navigation:
1. Hero: full-screen, cinematic. Headline in Turkish: “Sistemler Kurarım. Dönüşümü Yönetirim. Geleceği İnşa Ederim.” Subline: “Yönetici, girişimci ve yapay zekâ odaklı dönüşüm lideri.” Supporting sentence: “20 yıla yaklaşan operasyon, teknoloji, finansal kontrol ve çok lokasyonlu yönetim deneyimini; yapay zekâ, ERP ve yeni nesil iş modelleriyle birleştiriyorum.” Primary CTA: “Benimle Çalışın”. Secondary CTA: “Hikâyemi Keşfedin”. Add a strong portrait-image placeholder area that can later be replaced with Evren’s real photo. Visual motif: subtle blueprint grids, data lines, city lights, abstract AI network, and a premium executive silhouette.
2. About / Manifesto: title “Ben sadece şirket yönetmem. Sistem kurarım.” Include concise copy explaining that Evren identifies chaos, builds structure, aligns people, processes, finance and technology, and turns fragmented organizations into measurable systems.
3. Core positioning cards: “Liderlik & Operasyon”, “AI & Dijital Dönüşüm”, “ERP & Sistem Kurulumu”, “Büyüme & Marka”, “Gayrimenkul & İnşaat”, “Uluslararası İş Geliştirme”. Each card should have one sharp sentence.
4. Experience timeline: Electrical & Electronics Engineer; 20 years of experience; senior management; general management; multi-site operations; 40+ employees; Germany/Turkey/Europe; ERP and AI projects. Use factual, non-exaggerated wording.
5. Signature framework section titled “ORDU Framework” with five pillars: Observe, Reframe, Design, Unify, Scale. Explain briefly in Turkish.
6. Selected work / projects: BauERP, AIOS, AI-enabled construction transformation, multi-site retail operations, international real estate marketing, corporate process and governance systems. Use elegant cards and state that detailed case studies are available on request.
7. Thought leadership section: title “Fikirler. Sistemler. Gelecek.” with article placeholders for AI, leadership, operations, construction tech, personal transformation.
8. Personal dimension: title “Teknoloji kadar insanı da önemserim.” Mention leadership, resilience, fatherhood, photography, Formula 1, philosophy and continuous learning, with tasteful restraint.
9. Closing CTA: “Bir şirketi büyütmek, bir sistemi kurmak veya bir fikri gerçeğe dönüştürmek istiyorsanız konuşalım.” Buttons for LinkedIn, Email, WhatsApp placeholders.
10. Footer: Evren Ordu, Frankfurt / Germany, and small statement “Built around clarity, courage and systems.”

Add language switcher UI for TR / DE / EN, with Turkish active. The visible default content should be Turkish. Implement subtle scroll animations and interactive hover states, but keep performance high. Add sticky minimal navigation, section progress indicator, and a premium mobile experience. Use strong semantic structure and basic SEO metadata for “Evren Ordu”, “AI transformation”, “ERP”, “operations leadership”, “Germany”, “digital transformation”.

The site should look credible to recruiters, investors, founders, clients, and senior executives. The design should immediately communicate: strategic thinker, builder, leader, technologist, and storyteller. Do not use fake logos, fake testimonials, fake awards or inflated claims. Use placeholders where personal contact links or real photos are needed.
